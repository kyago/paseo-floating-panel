Floating panel adds a round button to the bottom-right of the Paseo desktop window. Clicking it opens a 360 by 480 panel; the dash in its header folds it back into the button. Drag the button or the panel header to move it; the position is remembered on that client.

The panel sits above Paseo's layout, so it stays visible when the sidebar is collapsed. It is hidden on phones and in windows narrower than 768 pixels. Colors follow the operating system's light or dark mode, not Paseo's theme setting. The panel lists the agents of every connected host by state: needs input, failed, running, and finished but unread, with finished sessions folded away. Selecting a row opens that session, and permission requests can be allowed or denied from the panel; questions are answered in the session. The button shows how many agents need input or failed.
